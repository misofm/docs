The [Miso Music Protocol](https://github.com/misofm/music-protocol) core is small. A composition identifies a written work, a recording points to a composition, and a release orders recordings. Each work has a fixed-supply share type. Licensing, royalties, credits, titles, artwork, and sales live in separate packages.
